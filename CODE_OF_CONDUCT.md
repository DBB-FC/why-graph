# Code of conduct

Why Graph is a small project built in the open, and people who use it come from many places and
speak different languages. English and Spanish are both welcome everywhere in this repository.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most bugs are reported by someone who just lost time.
- Critique the code, the design or the idea, never the person.
- Share what you know. A clear bug report or a reproducible vault is a gift.
- Respect other people's notes: never paste private content from your vault or anyone else's.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing
someone's private information, and any conduct that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may
block people who keep breaking them. To report something privately, write to
**hola@dontbuybuild.cl**. Reports are read by the maintainer only and kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
